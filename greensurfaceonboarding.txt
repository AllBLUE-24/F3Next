## 🔍 BUSINESS MODEL ANALYSIS

### **Green Surface is NOT a Simple Manufacturer**

They operate in **THREE DISTINCT MODES:**

**MODE 1: Chemical Manufacturing (Make-to-Stock)**
```
Raw Materials (Epoxy resins, hardeners, additives)
         ↓
Mixing/Formulation (batch production)
         ↓
Finished Products (packaged in containers)
         ↓
Stock Inventory (100+ SKUs)
         ↓
Sell to distributors/customers
```

**MODE 2: Project-Based Manufacturing (Make-to-Order)**
```
Client inquiry (Pharma company needs anti-bacterial flooring)
         ↓
Site survey and specifications
         ↓
Custom formulation (if needed)
         ↓
Material preparation for project
         ↓
Execute on-site installation
         ↓
Project delivery and warranty
```

**MODE 3: Trading (Buy-Sell)**
```
Buy raw materials (resins, hardeners, additives)
         ↓
Possibly sell some raw materials to other manufacturers
         ↓
Also buy finished products from other manufacturers to complete product range
```

## 🏗️ **F3ICHE: PROJECT MANUFACTURING**

**Definition:**
Businesses that BOTH manufacture products AND execute projects using those products.

**Characteristics:**
1. Own manufacturing (batch production of products)
2. Project-based sales (not just off-the-shelf sales)
3. Site execution/installation services
4. Hybrid inventory (stock items + project-specific materials)
5. Dual costing (product costing + project costing)

**Other Examples of Project Manufacturing:**
- Elevator manufacturers who also install elevators
- AC manufacturers who also do HVAC installations
- Solar panel manufacturers who also do installations
- Custom machinery manufacturers who also do commissioning

**ERPNext Modules Needed:**

## 📊 INDUSTRY CLASSIFICATION

**Primary Industry:** Specialty Chemicals - Construction Coatings  
**Sub-Industry:** Epoxy & Polyurethane Floor Coating Systems  
**Market Segment:** B2B Industrial + B2C Household

**Industry-Specific Requirements:**

### **1. Batch Manufacturing (Chemical Compliance)**
- Batch tracking mandatory (for quality control)
- Shelf life tracking (epoxy has limited pot life)
- Hazardous material handling (chemicals)
- Batch-wise costing (raw material costs vary)
- Quality testing records (per batch)

### **2. Project Management Requirements**
- Bill of Materials per project (site-specific)
- Material estimation and planning
- Labor/subcontractor tracking
- Equipment usage tracking (mixing machines, sprayers)
- Site-wise costing and profitability
- Work-in-progress tracking
- Project milestones and payments
- Warranty tracking

### **3. Compliance Requirements**
- GST registration (manufacturing + services)
- Factory license (for manufacturing facility)
- Pollution control clearance (chemical manufacturing)
- Quality certifications (ISO, industry-specific)
- Safety compliance (chemical handling)
- Project-wise GST accounting (different rates for goods vs services)

### **4. Stock Management Complexity**
- **Raw Materials:** Epoxy resins, hardeners, pigments, additives
- **Finished Goods:** Packaged products for sale
- **Project Materials:** Materials allocated to specific projects
- **Consumables:** Mixing tools, application equipment
- **Three valuation methods needed:**
  1. FIFO for finished goods
  2. Batch-wise for manufactured products
  3. Project-wise for allocated materials
