What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.

We are building an AI-powered implementation and operations engine that automates the deployment of ERP systems for MSMEs.

Currently, setting up an ERP is a bottleneck; it requires expensive, manual consultants to clean data, understand business context, and configure the software. Our product, F3Next, replaces this entire manual process.

How it works: A company simply uploads their raw, unstructured business data. Our AI ingests and analyzes this data to extract the company’s context and maps it to the correct schemas. Future Vision: While our wedge is automating ERPNext, our long-term mission is to build an agnostic automation harness. We will engineer a universal client that can interface with and automate the implementation and operation of any major ERP system on the market.

Our competitors fall into three distinct categories:

Legacy Systems Integrators (SIs) & Consultants: These are the traditional IT agencies and local implementors. They are our primary target to replace.

Native ERP AI Wrappers (e.g., Zoho Zia, Microsoft Copilot, SAP Joule): These are built-in assistants designed by the ERP companies themselves.

Generalist Autonomous Agents & MCP Servers (e.g., Claude Code, Devin): Highly capable AI agents designed to execute coding and software operations.

We understand that the true friction of ERP adoption isn't just navigating the software—it is the translation of messy, real-world business data into strict software constraints. Each competitor fundamentally misses this reality:

The Legacy Implementors profit from complexity: They sell billable hours. They have zero incentive to build a system that sets up an ERP in minutes because their business model relies on 6-month consulting contracts.

Native ERP AIs only work after the hard part is done: Tools like Zoho Zia are just conversational wrappers for perfectly structured data. They assume the 6-month, 5-lakh rupee implementation has already happened. They do not solve the "cold start" problem of migrating messy spreadsheets into the system.

Generalist Agents are built for developers, not factories: Claude Code and other MCP-based agents are expensive, highly generalized, and require technical prompt engineering. They lack the strict guardrails required for reliable enterprise operational logic (like double-entry accounting or supply chain mapping).

Our Secret: We know that MSMEs will not adapt their workflows to fit software; the software must adapt to them. By starting from their raw, unstructured data and automating the data-mapping layer, we provide an affordable, perfect, and vertical-specific solution that SIs won't build, Native AIs can't handle, and Generalist Agents are too expensive to execute.

We operate on a B2B SaaS model built on a "Land and Expand" strategy. While we haven't finalized our exact pricing structure yet, our current model is built around drastically lowering the barrier to entry:

The Wedge (Base Subscription): We plan to charge a highly disruptive base subscription, starting around ₹999/month (~$11). Traditional ERP implementations cost upwards of ₹5 lakhs upfront and take months to deploy. By offering an instant, AI-driven setup for ₹999, we transform a massive, risky capital expenditure into a no-brainer operational expense for MSMEs.

Tiered Expansion: As businesses grow and rely more heavily on our architecture, we will offer premium tiers (e.g., ₹4,999/month and up) for larger organizations. These tiers will unlock multi-tenant environments, advanced multi-agent workflows, and priority support.

Usage-Based Upsells: Base plans will include a set limit of AI operations (e.g., 300 prompt commands per month). We will monetize heavy compute usage—such as bulk parsing of massive legacy databases or high-frequency prompt-to-action queries—through add-on API credits.

Currently, our core architecture uses Gemini Flash, keeping our raw compute costs per user under $5/month, which yields a gross margin of over 65% even on our cheapest ₹999 tier.
